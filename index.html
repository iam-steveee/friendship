A note from me to you

Hi, there.

I am Steve. That's not my real name, but you can call me that.

If I shared this link with you, it probably means I'd like to be friends with you, or at least get to know you a little better.

Why friends? Well, I'm on a MISSION, collecting friends and building an army so I can get your vote and become Prime Minister with your help...

Awww, just kidding. LOL.

But seriously, I just want people I can talk to about random things, laugh with over the stupidest jokes, and share little moments with. I'm a keyboard warrior, so be warned, I write LONG paragraphs. Sometimes I've got too much to say, and sometimes I just like making people feel special.

I don't expect you to reply every second or put me before everyone else in your life. I know you've got your own world to live in. I just hope that, even on your busiest days, you'll sometimes think of me and feel like talking to me.

Honestly, the little things mean a lot to me. A random text, a silly joke, or a simple "hey, how are you?" can make my entire day. You might forget you even sent that message, while I might find myself smiling at my phone like an idiot. That's just how I am.

And bruhh, I care too much and get attached way too easily. Someone's gotta fix me. 😭 I might need a therapist, and I'm starting to think it could be you. LOL.

Jokes aside, when someone makes me feel comfortable enough to be myself, I start caring about them. I look forward to their messages, remember little things about them, and wonder how their day went. Before I even realise it, someone who was once a stranger becomes someone whose presence makes my days a little brighter.

Sometimes, I overthink whether I'm annoying someone, texting too much, or caring more than they do. But I don't want to stop caring just because I'm afraid of being disappointed. I like checking up on people, listening to their problems, and remembering the little things they might think nobody notices. I guess I just want to make people feel as appreciated as I hope to feel someday.

The truth is, I haven't really been able to make many friends in real life. I'm an introvert and an overthinker, and sometimes I have so much to say but don't know who to say it to. So, I'm trying here, hoping to meet people I can laugh with, talk to freely, and slowly build something genuine with.

I don't need hundreds of people who know my name. I'd rather have a few who know the person behind it. People I can be myself around, who don't mind my ridiculously long paragraphs, and who know they don't have to pretend to be okay around me, either. We can laugh about the stupidest things one day and talk about life the next.

I'm not looking for promises that we'll be friends forever. Life changes, people get busy, and sometimes things don't work out. I just hope that while we're part of each other's lives, we'll genuinely want to be there for one another. No forced conversations, no pretending, and no feeling like we're only talking out of obligation.

I can't promise to be a perfect friend. I'll probably overthink things, send random messages, tease you, and turn three sentences into three paragraphs. But if I call you my friend, I want it to mean something. I'll try to listen, care, and make you feel that your presence in my life matters.

And if you're reading this right now, maybe you're someone I'll be glad I met. Who knows? We might become close friends, or we might just have a few nice conversations. Either way, every friendship starts somewhere, right?

So, please don't close this page and forget about me immediately. 😭 I put a little piece of myself into this letter, and I hope it made you smile.

You don't need a perfect opening line or something clever to say. A simple hello is enough. We can figure out the rest as we go.

And who knows? Maybe this little letter will be the beginning of a friendship neither of us expected.

✦

You came here as someone who knows a little about me. Maybe you'll leave as someone who knows me a little better.

Your friend,

Steve
